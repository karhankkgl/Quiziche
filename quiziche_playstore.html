📝 About This App

Quiziche is a fast-paced, real-time quiz battle game where your knowledge is your weapon! Challenge players around the world or invite your friends to a private room and see who reigns supreme across dozens of knowledge categories.

Whether you're a science genius, a history buff, or a pop-culture fanatic — Quiziche has the perfect category for you. Earn coins, climb the ELO leaderboard, complete Global Missions, and customize your profile avatar as you rise through the ranks.

With a sleek dark-themed UI, smooth animations, and a cartoon-style design that makes learning fun, Quiziche delivers a premium quiz experience unlike any other. Play solo to sharpen your skills, or jump into a multiplayer battle for ultimate bragging rights!

💰 Monetization Plan

Free-to-Play with Optional In-App Purchases

Quiziche is completely free to download and play. All core features — matchmaking, solo mode, leaderboard, and missions — are accessible without payment. The monetization model is designed to be fair and non-pay-to-win.

  • 🪙 Coin Packs — Players can optionally purchase coin bundles to speed up avatar unlocks and cosmetic items. Coins are also freely earned by playing games and completing missions.
  • 🎨 Cosmetic Items — Premium avatar frames and profile badges available via the in-game shop. These are purely decorative and do not affect gameplay.
  • 🚀 Quiziche Pro (Future) — A planned optional subscription that removes ads (if introduced), provides a "Pro" profile badge, and grants a small monthly coin bonus.
  • 📢 Ads — Minimal, non-intrusive banner ads may appear in menus. Pro subscribers can opt out. No forced video ads during gameplay.
🔒 Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

Quiziche takes user privacy seriously. We collect only the minimum data required to provide our services:

  • Account Information — Email address and display name, collected at registration via Firebase Auth (email/password or Google Sign-In).
  • Gameplay Data — Match results, scores, ELO ratings, and mission progress stored in Firebase Firestore to power leaderboards and profiles.
  • Device Information — Anonymous device identifiers for crash reporting and analytics (non-personally identifiable).
  • No Payment Data Stored — All in-app purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not store any payment or card information.

Users may request deletion of all personal data at any time by contacting: privacy@quiziche.app
